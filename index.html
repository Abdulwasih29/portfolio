Who I am

Curious by nature,
precise by training.

I'm a student of Agricultural and Bioresources Engineering at the Federal University of Technology, Minna, but engineering is only half the story.

The other half lives in philosophy, dark humour, the way a sentence can hold an entire argument, and the belief that curiosity is the only self-sustaining engine of progress.

I write. I build. I question things that most people agree to leave unquestioned. And I do it all with a kind of stubborn precision that tends to annoy the right people.
